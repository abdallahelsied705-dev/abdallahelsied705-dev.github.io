Portuguese IPTV Guide: Eight Services for Sport TV, Novelas and Brazilian Channels

Updated 8 November 2026

Portuguese households rarely compare IPTV on price alone; the questions are whether Liga Portugal matches are stable, whether RTP, SIC and TVI are all there, and whether Brazilian channels are included. Here are eight services built for Portuguese viewers.

Portuguese services

IPTVBR

IPTVBR — Best overall in Portugal. A single month costs €12.99, and the longest plan brings it down to about €4.17/month on the 12 months plan. Screens: Choose 1 or more devices. Trial: Free 24-hour trial on WhatsApp. Refunds: Requests are reviewed case by case; no fixed refund window is stated.

  • €49.99 a year (about €4.17 a month)
  • Free 24-hour trial
  • Prices in euros, site in Portuguese
  • Keep in mind: No fixed refund window

IPTV PT

IPTV PT — Best refund terms in Portugal. A single month costs €12.99, and the longest plan brings it down to about €4.17/month on the 12 months plan. Screens: Choose 1 or more devices. Trial: Test before you buy; length not stated. Refunds: 7 days after activation for a technical problem support cannot fix; immediate refund if activation fails.

  • 7-day refund for unfixable technical problems
  • Same low prices: €12.99 a month, €49.99 a year
  • Keep in mind: No refund for a simple change of mind

IPTV Listas

IPTV Listas — Best for 2 to 4 screens in Portugal. A single month costs €12.99, and the longest plan brings it down to about €4.17/month on the 12 months plan. Screens: 1 to 4 screens (price calculator on the site). Trial: Free 24-hour trial, no card. Refunds: No refund once your login has been sent.

  • Calculator for 1 to 4 screens
  • Free 24-hour trial without a card
  • 24/7 WhatsApp support
  • Keep in mind: No refund after activation, so use the trial

Melhor IPTV Listas

Melhor IPTV Listas — Best for football in Portugal. A single month costs €12.99, and the longest plan brings it down to about €4.17/month on the 12 months plan. Screens: Up to 4 devices at once. Trial: Free 24-hour trial, no card. Refunds: No refund policy page is linked on the site.

  • Free 24-hour trial without a card
  • Up to 4 devices at once
  • Keep in mind: Refund terms are not published

IPTV em Portugal

IPTV em Portugal — Best for free server switches. There is no one-month plan; the best value works out at about €5.17/month on the 12 months plan. Screens: 1 to 3 devices (3 devices: €150 a year). Trial: Free test; length not stated. Refunds: Policy page: refund only for a proven technical failure or billing error.

  • Free server switch if a channel is missing
  • Plans for up to 3 devices
  • Keep in mind: Refund wording differs between the homepage and the policy page
  • Keep in mind: No one-month plan

Golden Gate IPTV Portugal

Golden Gate IPTV Portugal — Best for 2 to 3 devices in Portugal. A single month costs €15, and the longest plan brings it down to about €5.17/month on the 12 months plan. Screens: 1 to 3 devices (2 devices: €94 a year, 3 devices: €150 a year). Trial: Free test on request. Refunds: 7 days from activation for new plans, after support tries to fix the problem.

  • 7-day refund on new plans
  • Clear prices for 1, 2 and 3 devices
  • Free server switch
  • Keep in mind: Renewals are not refundable

IPTV Portuguese

IPTV Portuguese — Easiest refund in Portugal. A single month costs €17, and the longest plan brings it down to about €5.00/month on the 12 months plan. Screens: 1 to 3 devices. Trial: Free test. Refunds: 7 days after activation if you are not satisfied (new plans); paid back by PayPal or card in 3 to 10 working days.

  • Refund within 7 days if you are not satisfied
  • Works with free apps like IPTV Smarters Pro and VLC
  • Keep in mind: One month costs more, at €17

FranciscoPresidente.net

FranciscoPresidente.net — Best two-year plan in Portugal. A single month costs €12.99, and the longest plan brings it down to about €5.38/month on the 24 months plan. Screens: Choose the number of devices at checkout. Trial: Free 24-hour trial, no card. Refunds: 48 hours if the service does not work as described; pro-rated refund if a fault is not fixed within 72 hours.

  • 24 months for €129
  • Free 24-hour trial
  • Card, PayPal or crypto
  • Keep in mind: Yearly plan costs more than IPTVBR's

Before paying for a year

  • Watch a big match live during the trial.
  • Check the guide on the main Portuguese channels.
  • Read the refund page itself, not just the homepage.

The best service is the one that holds up on your connection when the whole country is watching the same game.
